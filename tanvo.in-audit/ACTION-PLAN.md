2. Verify `https://www.tanvo.in/favicon.ico` and the new landing page return HTTP 200.
3. Add the domain to Google Search Console, submit `https://www.tanvo.in/sitemap.xml`, inspect the homepage and landing page, and request indexing.

## Next 30 days

1. Publish two real client case studies with permission: client, problem, scope, screenshots, launch date, quote, and measured outcome where verifiable.
2. Publish an About page with named founders/team members, roles, experience, real photos and LinkedIn profiles.
3. Publish a Contact page with one canonical public phone, hours, full service area and a direct GBP directions/profile link.
4. Ask completed clients for genuine Google reviews; respond to every review. Do not buy, gate or fabricate reviews.
5. Create complete profiles on reputable, relevant directories such as Clutch and GoodFirms only with truthful details and real work.

## Months 2–3

1. Build distinct service pages for custom web applications, e-commerce, UI/UX and SEO only when each has useful original content and proof.
2. Publish one evidence-led article per month: a local website teardown, an anonymised performance benchmark, a project postmortem, or a practical Hubli-Dharwad/Karnataka website cost guide.
3. Earn local mentions from clients, partners, the Hubballi-Dharwad business/startup ecosystem, industry associations and legitimate local press.
4. Add Karnataka-specific content only when there is distinct regional proof. Do not clone city pages or create doorway pages.

## Measurement

Track weekly in Search Console: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, average position and queries containing `Hubli`, `Hubballi`, `Dharwad`, `Karnataka`, `web development`, `website agency`, and service variants. Track GBP calls, website clicks and direction requests. Review target ChatGPT/Perplexity/Copilot prompts monthly, but treat answers as observations rather than stable rankings.

First realistic objective: local long-tail visibility and map-pack eligibility in Hubli-Dharwad. Karnataka is the next authority tier. The national `web development agency India` head term is a long-term outcome, not an immediate technical target.
